Upload Nursery and Primary Pupils
Excel / CSV Format

Your file should contain these columns:

  • full_name — required
  • parent_phone — optional
  • residence — optional

Residence must be Day or Boarding. If omitted, the pupil's residence will be left unset for later assignment.

Sample CSV Format 
full_name,parent_phone,residence
John Doe,0777123456,Day
Jane Smith,0788123456,Boarding
Peter Okello,0700123456,Day
Important:
  • Pupils are automatically uploaded into your assigned class.
  • The class assignment is not changed by the uploaded file.
  • Nursery classes include Baby, Middle and Top.
  • Primary classes include Primary 1 through Primary 7.
  • Do not include a programme column.
  • The bursar can configure fees according to class, residence, term and academic year.
Accepted formats: Excel (.xlsx, .xls) and CSV (.csv).
